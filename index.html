The café

A family-run room on the High Street.

One Shot Café sits in the middle of Albrighton — a long, bright room with reclaimed wood tables, Edison bulbs strung down the ceiling, and a mug of coffee painted on the wall at the far end.

The kitchen cooks everything to order, which is why the bottom of the menu politely warns you there may be a wait when it's busy. It's worth it. Coffee is Marabans, the cakes change on the counter, and when the sun's out there's a courtyard round the back.

  • Breakfast, brunch and lunch served all day, right up to close
  • Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options across the menu
  • Kids' menu for under-12s, with a free squash
  • Dogs and pushchairs welcome — order at the counter when you're ready
